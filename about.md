# Showcase Reborn

Showcase Reborn is a fan-made continuation inspired by the original Showcase mod. The original mod has not been updated for the current version of Geometry Dash, so I made this project to bring a similar experience back for newer versions of the game.



This mod is not affiliated with or endorsed by the creator of the original Showcase mod, and full credit for the original idea goes to them. I am not trying to replace or take ownership of their work. If the original creator wants this project renamed, changed, or taken down, they can contact me and I will respect their request.



Showcase Reborn is still being developed, so bugs and unfinished features may be present. Suggestions and bug reports are appreciated.



# How-To

Just open up a menu and you will see the button in the corner of the play button. One last thing: The button is a search button because this is a beta mod and im still trying to figure out how to make it with a custom image
